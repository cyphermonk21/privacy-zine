# Print Guidelines

## For Home Printing (Inkjet / Laser)

- **Paper size:** A4 (210 × 297 mm) or US Letter (8.5 × 11")
- **Double-sided:** Yes — flip on short edge
- **Color:** Full color preferred; grayscale acceptable
- **Quality:** 300 DPI minimum
- **Paper weight:** 80 gsm minimum, 100+ gsm for durability

## For Professional / Offset Printing

- **Bleed:** 3 mm on all sides
- **Safe zone:** Keep all content 5 mm from trim edge
- **Color profile:** Convert to CMYK (FOGRA39 or SWOP depending on region)
- **Export format:** PDF/X-4
- **Fonts:** Embedded as subsets
- **Resolution:** 300 DPI for raster elements
